How a question becomes a working form.

Question→Decision→Working form→Limit

Start from a human problem. Bound it to one decision. Give that decision a form that can be inspected: a product flow, a model, a prototype or an experiment. Then state what the work does not show yet, and the next validation step where there is one.

What is mine. I am not an engineer by training. I define the problem, write the requirements, direct AI coding agents and local language models through the build, check the result, and revise when the evidence shows a limit. The code is written with AI tools. The direction, the checks and the decisions are mine.

Go to the source
I take questions directly to suppliers, professors and specifications rather than rely on summaries.
Build it to know it
I produce a model, a prototype or a requirements document, so there is something real to test.
Grade my certainty
I record what the evidence supports and, just as clearly, what it does not.
